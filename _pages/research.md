## Multimodal Intelligence

I am interested in how models reason across visual and linguistic evidence, with a focus on vision-language models and multimodal large language models.

`Multimodal LLMs` · `Vision-Language Models` · `Reasoning`

## Trustworthy AI

I study how modern AI systems can communicate uncertainty and remain reliable under distribution shifts and challenging evaluation settings.

`Uncertainty Estimation` · `Calibration` · `Robustness` · `Reliability`

## AI for Healthcare

I am interested in developing and carefully evaluating multimodal AI systems for medical applications, where calibration, transparency, and responsible use are especially important.

`Medical AI` · `Multimodal Learning` · `Evaluation`

Research projects, publications, and collaborators will be added here as the work develops.
