About

About me

I am an undergraduate student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). My research interests sit at the intersection of multimodal learning, reliable machine learning, and medical artificial intelligence.

I am particularly interested in trustworthy multimodal intelligence: models that can connect visual and linguistic evidence, express uncertainty appropriately, and support careful evaluation in real-world applications.

Research

Questions I care about

My interests center on capable multimodal models, the evidence behind their predictions, and their responsible use in healthcare.

Multimodal Intelligence

Studying reasoning, perception, and representation in vision-language models and multimodal large language models.

  • Multimodal LLMs
  • Vision-Language Models
  • Reasoning

Trustworthy AI

Studying uncertainty estimation, calibration, evaluation, robustness, and the reliability of modern AI systems.

  • Uncertainty Estimation
  • Calibration
  • Reliability

AI for Healthcare

Developing and evaluating multimodal AI systems for medical applications, with an emphasis on responsible use in high-stakes settings.

  • Evaluation
